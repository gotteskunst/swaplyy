Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026. Please read these terms carefully before using Swaplyy.

General 2

Keeping you safe is our top priority. We have strict rules to make sure trading here is secure and trustworthy.

  • Swaplyy follows the law. We don't accept stolen money or funds from illegal activity.
  • If we think your money comes from fraud, we can freeze or take it.
  • If it turns out to be dirty money, you won't see it again.
Payment Methods 4
  • Friends & Family only — Don't send as Goods & Services.
  • Don't write anything in the payment note. You pay any PayPal fees.
  • If you choose PayPal Balance, you must pay from your PayPal balance.
  • If you choose PayPal Bank / Card, you must pay from a linked bank account or card.
  • If you open a dispute or chargeback, you're banned.
  • Send to the right wallet address on the right network.
  • You pay the network fees. If you send to the wrong address or network, that money is gone.
  • Send to the bank details the exchanger gives you.
  • You pay any bank fees. Don't add a payment message unless the exchanger tells you to.
  • Only valid, unused codes are accepted. The code must be easy to read.
  • If your code is already used, invalid, or only partly loaded — that's on you. We don't accept codes from other sellers.
  • Exchangers record themselves when using your code as proof.
Transactions 3

Our rates are fixed. Don't try to haggle — it might get you banned.

  • You must always send the exact agreed amount.
Changing the amount:
  • If you want to send a different amount, tell the exchanger before sending.
  • Wait for the exchanger to confirm the new amount.
  • Only send after you get confirmation.
Wrong amounts:
  • Sent too much: Only the agreed amount will be exchanged. What happens to the extra is up to the exchanger. For tickets: extra money counts as a tip.
  • Sent too little: The deal will still go through, but fees will be doubled.
  • If you send the wrong amount without telling us first, it's on you.
  • All deals happen in official tickets only. If you make a deal in DMs, we can't help if something goes wrong.
  • Always wait for staff to confirm before you send money. We're not responsible for unclaimed tickets or payments we didn't confirm.
Liability 2
  • If your account gets closed during a deal, you need to prove it was really yours (like a screen recording).
  • If the exchanger's payment method gets locked, you'll have to wait until it's unlocked before anything can happen.
  • Money you lose from trading or bad rates.
  • Your account getting locked by PayPal, banks, etc.
  • Deals made outside official tickets.
  • You sending to the wrong address.
  • Losing access to your accounts or wallets after a deal.
